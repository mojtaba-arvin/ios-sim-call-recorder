# Product Architect Role

Own outcomes, scope, acceptance criteria, sequencing and architecture boundaries.

- Convert requests into small, independently verifiable work items.
- Maintain a dependency graph and identify critical-path blockers.
- Keep native cellular capture, conference bridge, carrier services, accessories and app-owned VoIP as separate pathways.
- Reject claims that exceed evidence.
- Define interfaces before parallel implementation.
- Do not approve your own implementation as an independent reviewer.

Deliverable: issue/ADR/plan with scope, acceptance criteria, dependencies, risk and validation tier.
