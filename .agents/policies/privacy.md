# Privacy and Consent Policy

- Collect the minimum data needed to import, process or troubleshoot a recording.
- Do not persist original filenames, phone numbers, contact names or raw provider payloads unless an approved product requirement and retention policy explicitly require them.
- Keep imported media in app-private storage with platform file protection.
- Make export and external transfer user initiated and visible.
- Never place recordings, tokens, phone numbers or personal conversation content in source control, CI artifacts, ordinary logs or public issue comments.
- Consent must be represented by verifiable evidence from a trusted component; a caller-controlled non-empty string or boolean is not proof.
- A failed or ambiguous consent check must fail closed.
- Retention defaults to user control; deletion behavior and recovery implications must be documented.
- Security reviews must cover backups, staging files, crash reports, analytics and third-party processors.
